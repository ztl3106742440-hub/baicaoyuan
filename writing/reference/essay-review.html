万能加分句 · 主背八句

小作文优先取一两句，大作文取两三句。先看这句能不能完成题目要求，再决定用在哪一段。加粗的英文可以原样背。方括号里按题干填动作、人物或结果。时间地点、题内数据和具体例子用短句交代即可。

小作文 · 四句

建议主体 · 先做什么、重点看什么

I would start by [动名词短语], paying particular attention to [重点].

我建议先……，特别留意……。第一空填行动，第二空填从这个行动中要观察或决定的地方。接下去另写一步，用到前一步收集的信息。

I would start by comparing recent job postings, paying particular attention to the skills employers ask for.

2021 求职　先比较招聘信息，特别留意雇主要求哪些技能。

I would start by building your talk around one of his achievements, paying particular attention to the problem he was trying to solve.

2024 口头报告　先围绕一项成就组织讲稿，特别留意它解决了什么问题。

活动主体 · 介绍安排并写出收获

The programme will feature [具体环节], giving [谁] an opportunity to [动词原形短语].

活动包含……，让……有机会……。先给看得见的环节，再写受邀者、观众或参赛者实际能做的事。

The programme will feature demonstrations of student prototypes, giving each team an opportunity to explain its design to the judges.

2022 创新赛　参赛队展示原型，并向评委解释设计。

The programme will feature live robot demonstrations, giving visitors an opportunity to see how a robot detects obstacles.

科技展迁移示例　观众看机器人演示，了解它如何检测障碍。对方如果要担任评委或带队，再给一句明确的角色要求。

招募主体 · 从职责推出资格

As the role involves [职责，动名词短语], applicants should be able to [资格，动词原形短语].

由于这个岗位需要……，报名者应能……。前面交代实际任务，后面写完成它需要的能力。结尾再补向谁报名、提交什么、何时截止。

As the role involves speaking to students and handling their responses, applicants should be able to ask clear questions and keep accurate records.

2023 招研究助手　岗位要采访学生并处理答复，因此需要能清楚提问、准确记录。

As the role involves guiding visitors around the exhibition, applicants should be able to give clear directions and answer basic questions.

科技展迁移示例　岗位要引导访客，因此需要能指路、回答基本问题。

推荐主体 · 写清为什么值得

What makes [事物] worth [动名词] is [具体特点], giving [谁] [收获，名词短语].

……值得做，是因为……，让……能得到……。特点要帮读者做选择。书适合读书会，就写读者能拿什么来讨论；景点适合参观，就写能看到什么。

What makes this book worth reading is the way it asks simple questions about friendship and responsibility, giving us a clear starting point for discussion.

2015 读书会　书提出的友情和责任问题，能给读书会一个具体的讨论起点。

What makes the city wall worth visiting is the view it offers over the old city, giving you a clear sense of how the streets are laid out.

2017 推荐景点　登上城墙能看到老城布局，推荐理由来自亲眼看得到的体验。

大作文 · 四句

论点一 · 写明价值来自哪里

The value of [做法或条件] lies in the opportunity it gives [谁] to [动词原形短语].

……的价值在于它给……做……的机会。用单数事物或动名词短语填第一空。再用一句写这个机会实际怎么产生。

The value of regular practice lies in the opportunity it gives young rowers to learn skills that might otherwise be lost.

2023 龙舟　定期合练给年轻队员学会可能失传技艺的机会。

The value of a nearby park lies in the opportunity it gives residents to exercise without making a long trip.

2024 公园　公园离家近，居民有机会在少花路上时间的情况下锻炼。

论点二 · 写出活动怎样给人创造条件

[做法或场所，单数] also creates a setting in which [谁] can [动词原形短语].

……也提供了一个让……可以做……的情境。动作要跟第一点区分开。写完这句，接一个分工、相约、互动或共同练习的细节。

The race also creates a setting in which neighbours of different ages can work towards a shared goal.

2023 龙舟　不同年龄的村民为了同一个目标协作，后面可以写筹备中的分工。

Reading together also creates a setting in which a child can repeat the behaviour until it becomes familiar.

2016 以身作则　一起阅读给孩子反复练习、形成习惯的机会。

负面题 · 从一个行为推到后续影响

One less obvious consequence of [动名词短语] is that [直接影响], which in turn [下一层影响].

……一个较容易被忽略的后果是……，这进而会……。先写第一次变化，再写它造成的下一个结果。which 指前面发生的整件事，例句后面的动词用第三人称单数。

One less obvious consequence of checking a phone at the table is that speakers may cut their stories short, which in turn reduces the chances for others to respond.

2015 聚餐看手机　别人一直看手机，讲故事的人可能提前结束，其他人也少了继续交流的机会。

One less obvious consequence of putting off an assignment is that difficulties may emerge too late, which in turn leaves students less time to seek help.

2020 拖延的反向写法　发现困难时已经太晚，能留给求助的时间更少。

图表主体 · 提出一个有分寸的解释

One plausible explanation for [题内变化或现象] lies in [可能原因，名词短语].

对……的一种合理解释在于……。首段写题内事实，主体用这句提出推测。下一句补一个实际情境，说明这个原因怎样起作用。

One plausible explanation for the sharper rise in air conditioner ownership lies in the need for cooling in separate rooms.

2025 家电趋势　空调拥有量增长较快，一种可能的原因是不同房间有各自的制冷需求。

One plausible explanation for the prominence of safety lies in the risk of relying on a device when no caregiver is nearby.

2026 养老机器人　安全排首位，可能与无人看护时用设备的风险有关。接受度和关注点各有自己的统计口径。

2024 古代科学家口头报告

题干抓手　Paul 问的是怎样准备口头报告。举一项张衡的成就，给选材料、组织报告和检验讲解是否听得懂的建议。

Dear Paul,

For your oral report, you could introduce Zhang Heng and his work on detecting distant earthquakes.

I would start by building your talk around one of his achievements, paying particular attention to the problem he was trying to solve. You could ask how people detected earthquakes they could not feel. Once you have drafted an outline, rehearse the talk with a classmate so that you can see which parts still need explaining.

A simple diagram might help you explain the idea. I’d be happy to listen to a practice run if you like.

Best,
Li Ming

2023 龙舟与文化传承

材料抓手　画面有人赛龙舟、有人观赛。观众的话指出村里的比赛越来越热闹。论证技艺传承和社区协作。

In the drawing, young people are racing dragon boats while a crowd watches from the bank. A spectator remarks that the village race is becoming livelier, drawing attention to the role of local participation in keeping a tradition alive.

The value of regular practice lies in the opportunity it gives young rowers to learn skills that might otherwise be lost. By training alongside experienced rowers, beginners can learn to keep time and respond to one another’s movements. The race also creates a setting in which neighbours of different ages can work towards a shared goal. Organisers set the timetable, volunteers prepare the venue, and crews get ready to race. The event depends on people coordinating these distinct tasks.

Communities can keep the tradition accessible by offering short practice sessions for beginners and inviting residents to help with preparations. New volunteers, for instance, can help with training sessions before taking on more demanding roles. These opportunities give people a reason to return and carry the tradition forward.

大作文

展开论点一 · 正面作用

What makes [具体做法或条件] particularly valuable is that [谁因此能做什么].

what 引出主语从句。换题后主要替换做法与收益。适合论述正面影响。下一句接过程或例子，比如写出新手跟谁练、先学会哪个动作。

  • 2019 登山
    What makes a short break particularly valuable is that it gives people time to recover some energy.
  • 2020 拖延
    What makes an early start particularly valuable is that it leaves room for revision.

展开论点二 · 换一个角度

A further consideration is [另一个因素], which can [产生新的作用].

第一个空填名词短语，第二个用动词原形。评估第一点和第二点能否分别成立。有公园近的便利，也可以有同伴鼓励的作用。写限制时同样能接这句。

  • 2018 选课
    A further consideration is workload, which can determine whether students have time to benefit from a difficult course.
  • 2026 养老机器人
    A further consideration is the cost of ownership, which can affect whether a family sees a device as a realistic option over time.

用一个例子支撑

A case in point is [人物或情境＋定语从句].

相当于“一个能说明这点的例子是……”。适合把例子压成一句。先知道上句要证明什么，再决定谁处在什么场景、结果如何。

  • 2020 拖延
    A case in point is a student who has enough time to ask a tutor about a difficult reading before completing an essay.
  • 2015 聚餐看手机
    A case in point is a diner who misses a friend’s uncomfortable expression after a joke because they are looking at a screen.

结尾把判断落到行动

The practical implication is that [谁] [should / could ＋具体行动].

承接前两点给措施。“谁”可以是学生、学校、社区或制造商。条件和措施要对应前文，后面可以补一句说明为什么可行。

  • 2012 剩水
    The practical implication is that we should ask what can still be done when a plan goes wrong.
  • 2025 家电
    The practical implication is that households should weigh the cost of running an appliance alongside its purchase price.

Yours sincerely,
Li Ming

The university could extend gym opening hours so that students with late classes have time to exercise. It could also offer short beginner sessions to help students who are unsure how to get started.
延长营业解决没时间，入门课解决不知道如何开始。适合 2014 改善学生体质建议。
大作文 · 2019 登山题怎么写两点
A brief rest can restore enough energy to make the next stage of a difficult task manageable. A companion can also help someone decide what to do next when the whole task feels overwhelming.
第一点是身体资源恢复，第二点是同伴帮忙判断下一步。迁移到学习压力时，可以用短暂休息恢复注意力，再请同学帮忙找出卡住的环节。

安全、成本和适用条件

Families may be more willing to try a device if they know who will respond when it fails. A trial can also show whether the device provides enough practical help to justify the cost.

首先判断故障有人回应，再判断是否值得花钱。适合养老科技和公共服务。小作文可把这组素材改成可执行的参与条件，比如 Children should be accompanied by an adult during the workshop. 对于活动、招募和通知，写明谁受什么限制。

真题与范文说明

真题范围为 2011–2026 年考研英语一，本稿选 Part A 和 Part B 各 12 篇。题干和材料抓手沿用按年份核对的英语一资料。题卡提炼任务，正式写作时看原题。小作文中自行补入的姓名、时间地点、活动细则和家书陈列情况属于范文设定。图表数值使用题目所给数据。英文为原创示范，正文词数不含小作文标题、称呼和落款。
